LostVille: Find Hidden Objects

A hidden-object casual game combined with light city-building mechanics. Players explore beautifully illustrated scenes, search for missing items, and use in-game currency to expand and upgrade the town.

My Role

2D Artist

Type

Commercial Project

Duration

7 months

My Tasks

  • Created optimized 2D assets and interactive objects for multiple core hidden-object levels.
  • Maintained a unified bright visual style and ensured overall graphic quality across backgrounds.
  • Enhanced player experience through detailed, high-contrast, and highly readable visual elements.
  • Collaborated closely with tech-art and developers for seamless asset integration inside Unity.
  • Worked with Midjourney for rapid asset ideation, sketch generation, and concept workflows.

Key Features

  • Hidden-object gameplay mixed with smooth town-building progression.
  • Highly detailed environments filled with hundreds of interactive and hidden assets.
  • Mobile-friendly, intuitive UI scaling and polished spatial navigation.
