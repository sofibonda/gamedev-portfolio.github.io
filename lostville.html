LostVille: Find Hidden Objects

A hidden-object casual game combined with light city-building mechanics. Players explore beautifully illustrated scenes, search for missing items, and use in-game currency to expand and upgrade the town.

My Team Role

2D Artist

Type

Commercial project

My Tasks

  • Maintained a unified visual style and overall graphic quality.
  • Enhanced player experience through detailed and readable visual elements.
  • Collaborated closely with developers to integrate art assets into the project.
  • Worked with Midjourney to generate concepts and ideas.

Key Features:

  • Hidden-object gameplay mixed with town-building progression.
  • Highly detailed scenes with interactive objects.
  • Mobile-friendly, intuitive interface and smooth navigation.
