Elevator Pitch

Based on the popular tabletop game "Bunker", Last Shelter is a 6-player 3D social deduction and cooperative survival game. Locked inside an underground shelter after a global catastrophe, players must verbally debate, analyze randomized character traits, and selectively disclose information to survive. With life-support systems strictly limited to sustain only two people, the group must vote to eliminate the least viable members before the automated self-destruction protocol triggers.

Level Design: Designing Constraints & Sightlines

By keeping players seated around the central table, the gameplay focuses entirely on verbal communication and behavioral analysis. Under these strict spatial constraints, level design shifts from traditional environment layout to managing player focus within a restricted First-Person POV.

Проверка ракурса с места игрока: оценка читаемости интерактивных объектов окружения.
Сетка сцены: радиальное распределение пропсов вокруг главного композиционного якоря.

Key Level Design Solutions:

  • Radial Room Geometry: Since players view the world from a first-person perspective with a limited viewing angle, I designed a specific polygonal wall layout. This radial geometry ensures that all critical perimeter assets (server racks, terminals, and sensors) remain perfectly readable and enter the player's line of sight smoothly just by rotating their view, completely eliminating visual occlusion or overlapping geometry.
  • Lighting Zoning (Unity 6): I utilized a high-contrast lighting setup to guide player attention. The central interactive table is highlighted with a bright, direct light source, while the perimeter workstations fade into deep shadows. This amplifies the claustrophobic atmosphere of isolation and psychologically anchors the group to the primary decision-making zone.
Финальный визуал в Unity 6: интеграция индустриальных ассетов, проработка износа поверхностей и финальный свет.
Окончательный вид сверху: строгая геометрия и баланс пространства, оптимизированные под low-poly стилистику.

Gameplay Mechanics: The Multi-Layered Balancing Matrix

To completely eliminate predictable meta-strategies (where players are exiled based on a flat, linear hierarchy of their professions), I developed a complex, multi-layered mathematical model called the Survival Index (SI) The final match outcome is evaluated algorithmically based on the traits of the surviving pair:

SI = Profession Base + Experience + Age + Health + Phobias + Character Traits + Cross-Character Synergies

Instead of relying on single-character stats, the gameplay economy forces players to deduce how their traits interact with others, shifting the core loop from simple voting to strategic social engineering.

Core Balancing Pillars:

  • High-Value Systemic Occupations: Occupations are weighted based on physical utility within an isolated bunker environment. High-priority professions like Doctors (+6) or Engineers (+6) provide critical long-term sustainability, while pure entertainment-focused roles like Actors (+1) or Artists (+1) offer minimal systemic weight.
  • Cross-Character Profession Synergies: To incentivize tactical disclosure, specific profession pairs unlock latent efficiency modifiers. For example, a surviving Doctor + Scientist pair yields a +2 synergy bonus, while an Engineer + Electrician pairing grants +2 to reflect systemic bunker optimization.
  • CPsychological & Behavioral Intersections: Character traits dynamically amplify or diminish individual scores through direct synergies and anti-synergies. A character with the Leader trait gets a +2 modifier when paired with a Reliable player, but if two players with the Leader trait attempt to co-survive, they generate a stiff resource-competition penalty.

UX & Onboarding Design

В условиях, когда на раунд обсуждения дается всего одна-две минуты, игрок должен моментально считывать тонну случайно сгенерированных текстовых данных. На этапе пре-производства в Figma я столкнулась со значительным информационным перегрузом (Information Overload) пользователей.

Итерации и оптимизация: В первых прототипах характеристики выводились списком, что путало игроков во время экспрессивного голосового чата. Я полностью переработала интерфейс, упаковав личные данные в формат стилизованного физического Личного Блокнота (Personal Notepad) с логическими вкладками, а для отслеживания чужих слов создала Книгу Наблюдения (Observation Book). Я лично интегрировала эти ассеты в Unity, настроив компоненты UI и связи в Инспекторе для сохранения идеальной четкости экранных форм на любых разрешениях.

Project Outcomes

The project was successfully defended as a Bachelor’s Diploma Thesis in 2026. As the Game & Level Designer, I delivered a fully functional 3D multiplayer prototype layout, a scalable balancing matrix handling hundreds of character variations, and comprehensive gameplay documentation.
