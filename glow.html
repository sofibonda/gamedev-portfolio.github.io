Glow of the Depths

PogJam 2026 Game & Level Design Unity 2D LevelUpCDV
My Team Role

Project Overview & Concept

"You are a diver trapped in an underwater cave after a boat accident. There is no communication, the way back is blocked, and your torch is broken. The darkness is only dispelled by the flickering of corals — but their light is gradually fading. To survive and find your way out, you must collect glowing corals to prolong the light and solve puzzles with sea creatures to uncover hidden passages. In the depths, there is no time to waste — only fragile light, the silence of the ocean, and the struggle for survival."

Developed during PogJam 2026 around the theme "Neon", Glow of the Depths is an atmospheric 2D stealth-puzzle game built around this core narrative premise. As the Solo Game & Level Designer, I turned these narrative constraints into explicit gameplay loops where light management, visibility, and color-coded pacing dictate survival.

Core Mechanics & Color-Coded Pacing

Light as a Decaying Resource:
Instead of using conventional UI timers or health bars, light management serves as the primary game loop. The player harvests crystals from bioluminescent corals to illuminate the surrounding cave. As the crystal's glow steadily diminishes, the visibility radius shrinks, creating psychological tension and forcing quick spatial decisions before complete darkness takes over.

4-Phase Color Progression:
To deliver a structured experience within tight jam limits, I designed the level pacing around four distinct color-coded mechanical phases:

  • Violet Corals (Onboarding): Teaches basic light decay timers, coral harvesting, and pathing in a safe environment.
  • Green Corals (Stealth & Cover): Introduces patrolling underwater predators and a kelp camouflage mechanic for stealthy passage.
  • Blue Corals (Escort Navigation): Features fast bioluminescent fish acting as dynamic guiding lights through completely unlit hazard zones.
  • The Abyss (Culmination): A dramatic spike in tension featuring an open-space escape from a giant apex predator toward the surface light.
Level Design Pre-production: Initial paper sketch mapping cave topology, branching paths, and hazard zones.
Unity 2D Scene Pass: Implementing non-linear cave pathways and high-contrast ambient lighting.

Production & Unity Implementation

Scope & Project Management:
With a 5-person team containing only one programmer, strict scope discipline was essential. As the Lead Designer and Project Manager, I prioritized core mechanics to prevent feature creep, keeping the team aligned through the 48-hour crunch.

Hands-on Unity Work:
To relieve programmer bottlenecks, I took on a hybrid Unity Generalist role. Beyond initial paper prototyping, I built environment layouts directly inside Unity, configured object hierarchies, and authored dynamic cinematic animations for key sequence events.

Project Summary

Glow of the Depths highlights rapid prototyping, dynamic resource-based level pacing, and effective cross-functional leadership under extreme time constraints.
